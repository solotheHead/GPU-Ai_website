7. How long we keep data

Retention depends on the purpose, the stage of your enquiry or contract, applicable legal retention duties and any current dispute. We do not treat every website record as an accounting document.

  • Enquiries that do not become a contract: our retention policy is to review the need to keep them at 12 months after the last substantive contact and delete or anonymise information no longer needed. An ongoing request or specific legal claim may justify longer retention. This is a review policy, not a claim that every website and email record is automatically deleted after 12 months.
  • Orders, service and warranty records: for the time needed to perform the contract and handle the applicable warranty or conformity period, and afterwards only for applicable statutory recordkeeping or limitation periods and unresolved claims.
  • Accounting and tax records: for the mandatory period applicable to the particular document under Bulgarian accounting and tax law, calculated from the starting point prescribed by that law.
  • Reviews: while published and relevant to the product, subject to a justified removal request, an objection or periodic review of continued need. Moderation and abuse evidence is retained only as needed for that purpose or a related claim.
  • Technical logs and backups: for the limited operational and security period applicable to the system, extended only where a specific incident or legal duty justifies it. Backups follow a rotation schedule; erased information must not be restored into active use without applying the relevant deletion again.
  • Consent choices and rights requests: as needed to apply your choice, handle your request and demonstrate the response or consent where required. Cookie lifetimes are explained separately below.

Where a legal duty or a concrete dispute requires retention, access and use are limited to that purpose. Data is deleted or effectively anonymised when no longer needed.

8. Cookies and similar storage

Cookies and similar technologies store information in your browser or device. Some support a service you request, such as a shopping basket, login or security check. Others remember an optional preference or support optional measurement or advertising. Session storage normally ends with the session; persistent storage remains until its expiry or deletion.

Strictly necessary storage does not require the same opt-in as optional tracking. Optional technologies requiring consent must remain inactive until the relevant choice is made. Refusing optional cookies must not prevent an ordinary enquiry or purchase. The review form’s option to remember your name, email and website address in this browser is optional; you can leave it unticked and delete the saved details through your browser.

We do not use solely automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you in our ordinary enquiry and hardware sales process. A technical configuration estimate or spam filter is not an acceptance of an order.
