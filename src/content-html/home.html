The practical details

Before we build.

How do I order a server?

Configure a platform and send an enquiry. We review compatibility, availability and requirements, then confirm the complete price, taxes, delivery and lead time in a written quotation.

Can you set up a GPU rental business?

Yes. The project can include hardware, software, marketplace onboarding, colocation or installation at your premises, plus agreed ongoing services. Rental income is variable and is not guaranteed.

Can you install in a data center or at my premises?

Either route can be included. Power, cooling, connectivity, access and installation work are assessed for the proposed location before deployment.

What warranty and support are included?

Your quotation identifies hardware cover and any component exceptions. Monitoring, maintenance, response targets and on-site work are agreed separately for your selected service package.
