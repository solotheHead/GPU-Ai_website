NVIDIA H200 NVL: price, specifications and servers

The NVIDIA H200 NVL is the PCIe version of the H200: 141GB of HBM3e memory with 4.8TB/s of bandwidth in a dual-slot card that fits standard GPU servers, with NVLink bridges to connect two or four cards. This page covers the specifications, how the NVL differs from the H200 SXM, our indicative EU prices for one to eight GPUs and what an H200 NVL server needs.

H200 NVL vs H200 SXM

Both have 141GB of HBM3e at 4.8TB/s. The difference is the form factor and the server around it.

H200 NVL H200 SXM
Form factor PCIe card, dual-slot SXM module on a GPU baseboard
Servers Standard PCIe GPU servers, up to 8 GPUs HGX H200 systems with 4 or 8 GPUs
GPU-to-GPU link NVLink bridges for 2 or 4 GPUs, 900GB/s per GPU NVLink at 900GB/s
Maximum power Up to 600W Up to 700W
FP8 Tensor Core 3,341 TFLOPS (with sparsity) 3,958 TFLOPS (with sparsity)
Multi-Instance GPU Up to 7 × 16.5GB Up to 7 × 18GB
NVIDIA AI Enterprise Included Add-on

The SXM version gives somewhat more compute per GPU, but it only comes in HGX systems built around a GPU baseboard — a different class of server. The NVL fits the same kind of 4U PCIe GPU server as other professional GPUs, which is what we build.

What an H200 NVL server needs

  • Airflow. The card is passively cooled and depends on the server’s fans, so the chassis must be designed for passive 600W GPUs.
  • Power. Eight cards can draw about 4.8kW for the GPUs alone, before processors, memory and storage. Plan the power and cooling of the room or colocation before you buy.
  • PCIe 5.0. To use the card’s PCIe Gen5 link, the platform needs PCIe 5.0 slots — for example AMD EPYC 9004-series (SP5) processors in our NX-G8 Dual SP5.
  • NVLink spacing. NVLink bridges need matching slot spacing between the linked cards.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the H200 NVL and the H100 NVL?

The H100 NVL has 94GB of memory; the H200 NVL has 141GB of HBM3e at 4.8TB/s. More memory lets larger models and longer contexts run on each card.

Do I need NVLink bridges?

Not for running separate models on each GPU. They help when one model is split across two or four cards, because the GPUs exchange data much faster than over PCIe. Bridges are confirmed and priced in your quotation.

Can the H200 NVL go in any server?

No. It needs a server designed for passive 600W GPUs with sufficient airflow and power. We confirm compatibility for your platform in the quotation.
