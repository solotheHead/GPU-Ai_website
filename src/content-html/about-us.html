ABOUT NODEX SERVERS

GPU infrastructure, from your first plan to deployment

NodeX Servers supplies GPU servers and custom graphics cards for AI compute, rendering and GPU rental projects. We bring hardware selection, system builds and deployment services into a proposal shaped around your workload, location and budget.

Discuss your project

Hardware and the work around it

Specify and build

Choose a server platform and GPU configuration with the memory, storage and networking your project needs. We review compatibility, power and cooling before the final specification is agreed.

Deploy in the right place

We can arrange colocation in Bulgaria or installation at your premises, with operating system, driver and platform setup included in the agreed scope.

Support continued operation

Monitoring, maintenance and troubleshooting can be arranged for your deployment. Responsibilities, recurring costs and service commitments are defined separately from the hardware warranty.

Building a GPU rental business

For customers planning to offer compute through platforms such as Vast.ai, we can help with hardware planning, deployment and marketplace setup. You own the equipment and choose the services you need from us. Platform approval, customer demand and operating costs remain part of the business decision; rental income and payback are not guaranteed.

A clear process before you commit

  1. Share the goal: intended workloads, budget, quantity, location and timing.
  2. Review the proposal: hardware, pricing, delivery, warranty and any installation or ongoing services.
  3. Agree the order: sales proceed by written quotation and acceptance.
  4. Build and deploy: we carry out the agreed assembly, checks and installation.
